This site is a personal CV. It does not use accounts, forms or advertising.

## Analytics

If you choose **Accept** on the banner, the site loads Google Analytics. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and sends usage data to Google, such as the pages you view, your approximate location and information about your device and browser. I use it only to see how many people read the site and which pages they read.

If you choose **Decline**, or do not respond, Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage so that the banner is not shown again. You can change it at any time with **Cookie settings** in the footer; declining after accepting also removes the Google Analytics cookies that were set.

## Fonts and icons

Fonts and icons are served from this site. No third-party font or icon service is used.
